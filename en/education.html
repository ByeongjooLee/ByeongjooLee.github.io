Textbook

Understanding Media & Digital Literacy — WikiDocs e-book2026 –
A textbook for building an integrated competence in the age of generative AI: reading media critically → verifying with data → expressing findings academically. Every concept is explained in plain language with everyday examples, accessible to high-school and university students with no prior background. It expands the integrated instructional model published in the KCI-indexed Journal of Literacy into an e-book with added theory, concepts, and exercises. (In Korean.)
